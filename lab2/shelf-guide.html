Shelf guide

A quick look at how long common dorm foods keep in the fridge, and what the dates on packages mean.

How long does it last?

These are general guidelines. Always follow the package, and see FoodKeeper on FoodSafety.gov for more foods.

FoodIn the fridge
Raw chicken or minced meat1 to 2 days
Cooked leftovers3 to 4 days
Opened lunch meat3 to 5 days
MilkAbout 7 days
Yogurt1 to 2 weeks
Eggs in the shell3 to 5 weeks

Reading the date on a package

Use by

This is the date to take seriously. Follow it, even if the food looks fine.

Best before

This is about quality. Food stored well is often fine after this date, so check it first.

Sell by

This date is for the shop. It does not tell you when food stops being good.
