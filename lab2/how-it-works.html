How Soonish works

Three habits and one shared list. Here is what happens to a box of food from the moment it goes in.

From shelf to plate

  1. Label. Write your name and the date on the food, and add it to the floor list.
  2. Watch. The list counts down the days and warns you two days before the date.
  3. Pass it on. If you will not eat it, mark it as up for grabs.
  4. Enjoy. A neighbour takes it, and the item leaves the list.

What makes Soonish different

Most food apps are built for one person. Soonish is built for a fridge with twenty owners.

Made for shared fridges

Each item shows its owner and its shelf, so people find it quickly.

Permission to share

The up-for-grabs status makes taking food polite instead of awkward.

Early warning

Two days of notice leaves enough time to cook, freeze or give food away.
