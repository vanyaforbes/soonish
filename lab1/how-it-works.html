How Soonish works

Three simple habits

Soonish does not need new equipment, only a marker and a shared page. A student adds food to the shared list, the list shows how many days are left, and the owner decides what to do before the date arrives.

Why neighbours can help

The most original part of Soonish is the status "up for grabs". It gives neighbours clear permission to take food. Sharing becomes polite instead of awkward, and food that one person cannot finish feeds somebody else.

The three steps

  • Label: write the owner and date on the food
  • Watch: see what must be eaten soon
  • Pass it on: mark food as free for the floor

Source: FoodKeeper storage advice from FoodSafety.gov
