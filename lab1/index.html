Soonish: the shared fridge that remembers

Why we chose this

In a student dorm, one fridge serves a whole floor. Food gets pushed to the back, its owner goes home for the weekend, and nobody else dares to touch it. Good food turns into waste only because nobody knows whose it is or when it expires.

What we propose

Soonish is a website for students who share a fridge. Each item gets an owner and a date. When something is close to its date, the owner is reminded, and if they will not eat it, they can pass it on to the floor before it spoils.

Who it is for

  • Students living in dorms with a shared kitchen
  • Floor leaders who keep the kitchen clean
  • Anyone who shares a fridge with flatmates

Source: UNEP Food Waste Index Report 2024
