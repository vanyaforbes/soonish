Food waste starts at home

A global problem

According to the UNEP Food Waste Index Report 2024, the world wasted about 1.05 billion tonnes of food in 2022, close to one fifth of the food available to consumers. Households were responsible for about 60 percent of it.

Why a dorm makes it worse

A shared fridge adds its own problems. There is no clear owner, no common way to mark dates, and people feel it is rude to touch food that is not theirs. Food waste also adds to climate change, so every saved meal counts.

What goes wrong in a shared fridge

  • Food has no name or date on it
  • Owners forget what they left at the back
  • Neighbours would eat it, but cannot ask

Source: UN Sustainable Development Goal 12
