Fridge rules

A shared fridge works when everyone agrees on a few rules. Print these and tape them to the door.

Four rules for the door

  1. Name and date. Unlabelled food can be treated as shared.
  2. Top shelf first. Keep ready food high and raw meat on the lowest shelf.
  3. Ask or check the list. Take only what is marked up for grabs.
  4. Sunday check. One person a week clears what has gone off.

Before a long weekend

Give it away

Mark anything you will not finish before you leave.

Freeze it

If your floor has a freezer, freeze cooked food before its date.

Leave it clean

Wipe up spills, because a clean fridge shows what is really inside.
