About Soonish

Soonish is a Web Programming student project built around one global challenge: food waste.

Our goal

We want a dorm floor to throw away less food without extra effort. The website explains the problem, teaches simple habits and gives students one shared place to see what is in the fridge.

It supports UN Sustainable Development Goal 12, responsible consumption and production.
