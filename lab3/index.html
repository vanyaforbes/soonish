Good food should not die at the back of the fridge

Soonish shows a whole dorm floor what is in the shared fridge, whose it is and how long it has left. If you will not finish something, pass it on before it spoils.

Open the fridge Why it matters

Three habits, one calmer kitchen

You do not need new equipment. You need clear labels and permission to share.

Label it

Every item gets an owner and a date, so nothing in the fridge is a mystery.

Watch the clock

The food closest to its date moves to the top of the list.

Pass it on

Mark food as up for grabs, and your neighbours can eat it without asking.
