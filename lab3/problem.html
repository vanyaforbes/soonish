Food waste starts at home

Most people picture supermarkets and restaurants, but homes waste more food than both together.

A global problem

According to the UNEP Food Waste Index Report 2024, the world wasted about 1.05 billion tonnes of food in 2022. That is close to one fifth of the food available to consumers.

Households were responsible for about 631 million tonnes, or 60 percent. Food service made up about 290 million tonnes and retail about 131 million tonnes.

The UN wants to halve this. SDG target 12.3 calls for halving per capita food waste at the retail and consumer levels by 2030.

Why a dorm makes it worse

A shared fridge has problems a family fridge does not. Many owners use it, and nobody can see the whole picture.

  • Food has no name or date on it.
  • Owners forget what they left at the back.
  • Neighbours would eat it, but feel they cannot ask.
  • Students leave for weekends and holidays.
